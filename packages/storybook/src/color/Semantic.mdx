- `foreground.on-X` sits on `background.X` and its `-hover` / `-pressed` states.
- Other foregrounds sit on the page backgrounds: `default`, `default-hover`, `default-pressed`, `subtle`, `raised`.
- Text needs **4.5:1**; borders and solid fills need **3:1**. `disabled`, `border.default` and `background.overlay` are exempt.
