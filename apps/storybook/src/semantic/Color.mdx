<Section title="Rules">
  - `canvas` is the page. `base` is the default of every other group; `subtle` is the weaker fill or line, `muted` the weaker text.
  - A content name is either a level (`base`, `muted`) or the background it sits on: `content.inverse.*` on `background.inverse.*`, an intent's `content.emphasis` on its `background.emphasis`, `content.subtle` on `background.subtle`.
  - Level content and borders sit on the page: `canvas`, `hover`, `pressed`, and `surface.*`.
  - `hover` and `pressed` are states of the fill next to them: `canvas` (transparent elements), `inverse.base`, and `danger`'s `emphasis`.
  - Text needs **4.5:1**; borders and solid fills need **3:1**. `disabled`, `border.subtle`, `utility`, and `always` are exempt. Each badge shows the lowest ratio among the pairs `npm run check` tests.
</Section>
