<Section title="Rules">
  - `background` is the page. `surface` sits on it; `inverse` is the flipped fill for primary buttons, tooltips, and snackbars.
  - Strength goes `subtle` < `base` < `strong` < `stronger`, away from the background. `strong` and `stronger` are the hover and pressed fills in every group.
  - `content/base` and `subtle` sit on the background and surfaces. `content/inverse` sits on a flipped fill: `inverse/*`, or an intent's `surface/base` and up.
  - Text needs **4.5:1**; borders and strong fills need **3:1**. `disabled`, `border/subtle`, `utility`, and `always` are exempt. Each badge shows the lowest ratio among the pairs `npm run check` tests.
</Section>
